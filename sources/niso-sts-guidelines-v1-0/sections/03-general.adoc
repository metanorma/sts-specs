== General

This document describes how ISO and IEC encode standards documents in NISO
STS 1.0. It covers document structure, content conventions, tables,
formulae, figures, terms and definitions, references, and amendments.

The guidelines apply to all stages of a publication — working draft,
committee draft, draft for enquiry, final draft, and published edition —
and to every ISO/IEC document type (International Standard, Technical
Specification, Technical Report, Publicly Available Specification,
International Workshop Agreement, Guide, amendment, corrigendum,
interpretation sheet, and system amendment).
