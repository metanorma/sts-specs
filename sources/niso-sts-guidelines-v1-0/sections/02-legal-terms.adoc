== Legal terms

Standards documents carry copyright, license, and provenance metadata in
their front matter. In NISO STS, this information is encoded using the
<<element-permissions,`<permissions>`>> element and its children
(<<element-copyright-statement,`<copyright-statement>`>>,
<<element-copyright-year,`<copyright-year>`>>,
<<element-copyright-holder,`<copyright-holder>`>>,
<<element-license,`<license>`>>).

For the full list of legal-term elements, see the Tag Library chapter on
front-matter metadata.
